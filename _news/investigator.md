Voyage On Board the National Marine Facility's RV Investigator!

QUT Centre for Robotics Research Engineer Vincent Free and I boarded the CSIRO RV Investigator for our research voyage to the Coral Sea !  🚢 

We performed a large number of Blue Robotics ROV surveys in different conditions, and worked alongside a group of amazing scientists led by Helen Pask from Macquarie University. Our experiments focused on studying the impacts of lasers on behaviour of marine life like fish and sharks.

Thanks so much to Jane Williamson from Macquarie University and Vincent Raoult from Griffith University for the invitation to join this exciting fieldwork.  Vinnie and my participation in the voyage wouldn't have been possible without support from the QUT Centre for Robotics and the QUT (Queensland University of Technology) School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics.
