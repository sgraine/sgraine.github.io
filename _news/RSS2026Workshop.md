Our workshop, "Mapping the Reef: Underwater 3D Reconstruction for Coral Ecosystems," has been officially accepted for #RSS2026 in Sydney! 🇦🇺
 
🪸 Coral reefs are some of the most biodiverse ecosystems on Earth, but monitoring them at scale remains a significant challenge for robotics. As climate change continues to impact our oceans, the need for scalable, repeatable, and automated reef monitoring has never been more urgent. We’ll be exploring the latest in underwater SLAM, 3D reconstruction, and field robotics to help bridge the gap between robotics and reef science.
