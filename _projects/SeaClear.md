I was invited as a Distinguished Lecturer to attend the Technical University of Munich [SeaClear2.0 Winter School](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/itr/events/winterschool/).  The Winter School was an international school with attendees from all over the world, with a three day schedule of talks, lab visits and activities around the theme _"Advancing Marine Robotics for a Cleaner Ocean"_. 
The Winter School was organised by teh Technical University of Munich and hosted at the Science Congress Center in Garching, Munich. 

My Lecture was on the topic: _"Underwater Computer Vision for Ecosystem Monitoring and Reef Restoration: Real-World Challenges and Label Scarcity"_.

I was honoured to be fully supported to travel from Australia to Germany to attend, and wish to thank the organisers and speakers: Stefan Sosnowski, Lucian Busoniu, Gerard Ciurana Caire, Nikolas Dahn, Ivana Palunko, Domagoj Tolic, Nathalie Bauschmann, Hatem Sultan.
